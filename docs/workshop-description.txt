[h1]Wuxia: The New Dawn [ALPHA / UNDER CONSTRUCTION][/h1]

[b]⚠️ Work in progress. Only download it if you want to test the little that exists so far.[/b]
Right now it is for testing only: expect bugs, things will change, and saves made with it may not work in future versions.

[b]⏸️ Development is currently paused.[/b] The mod is made with the help of Claude (AI), and I am waiting for my weekly limit to reset before continuing. My programming knowledge is limited, so updates depend on that.

Brings Wuxia/Xianxia cultivation to Order: The New Dawn. Your colonists can awaken, gather Qi and walk the path to immortality.

[h2]What you can test now[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Cultivation tab[/b] on the colonist sheet: realm, level and Qi.
[*][b]Meditation Cushion[/b]: colonists gain Qi while meditating in their free time.
[*][b]Alchemy[/b]: Small Cauldron, Taiyi Cauldron and Elixir Workbench, with pills, materials and recipes.
[*]Mod texts in [b]English or Portuguese[/b], chosen with a button on the Cultivation tab.
[*][b]Test mode[/b] is on: a "Test kit" button on the Cultivation tab gives pills and materials, and the mod's buildings are built instantly.
[/list]

[h2]Planned[/h2]
Breakthroughs between realms, weapons and armor, forging, sects and new enemies.
